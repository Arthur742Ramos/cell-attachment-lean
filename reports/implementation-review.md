## Constructed geometry and continuity

1. **Genuine quotient retained.** `Adjunction` defines the closed complex unit disk, the raw topological coproduct `X ⊕ (Σ i, Disk)`, and `Quot (AttachingRel f)` with Mathlib's quotient topology. The single relation constructor gives precisely the prescribed boundary identification. `quotientMap_isQuotientMap` is a proved quotient-map assertion. `NormalForm` is only an algebraic readout: no false continuity or replacement topology is attributed to it. Its representative lemmas prove that original points remain distinct and interior coordinates have singleton raw equivalence classes. `Embeddings` uses saturated raw preimages to establish the actual open interior embedding; it never assumes that the whole quotient map is open.

2. **Actual open cover.** `OpenCover` checks openness of all interiors and closedness of the full set of centers componentwise on the quotient-map preimage. Thus the neighborhood omitting centers and the interior open set genuinely cover the quotient, for arbitrary index families and without separation assumptions on X. `CoverPushout.coverPushout` applies the pinned ordinary all-object fundamental-groupoid open-cover theorem directly to these two subspaces and their actual inclusion maps.

3. **Jointly continuous deformation, with the quotient-product issue discharged.** `RawNeighborhood` identifies the punctured raw coproduct homeomorphically with the open preimage of the punctured neighborhood and proves its restricted map is a quotient map. `Radial` proves positivity, disk containment, boundary fixing, and joint continuity of the explicit radial expansion. `Retraction` descends polar retraction continuously only on that punctured domain. `Deformation.neighborhoodRadial_continuous` uses the pinned Mathlib `IsQuotientMap.continuous_lift_prod_right` theorem (`Mathlib/Topology/CompactOpen.lean`, lines 582–605): its locally compact factor is the unit interval. Consequently this is a genuine jointly continuous homotopy, rather than separate continuity at each time or an unjustified product-of-quotients assertion. The resulting homotopy equivalence has the actual neighborhood inclusion as forward map.

4. **Disk and annulus facts proved.** `Annulus` obtains open-disk contractibility from convex-ball geometry. For the punctured open disk it constructs a radial deformation to the circle of radius 1/2, which remains inside the open disk and fixes that half-circle. Polar normalization and half-circle inclusion give an actual homotopy equivalence. `CircleGenerator` derives the circle fundamental-group equivalence from the exponential covering and computes the concrete positive generator's image as 1; integer-power generation is proved from that equivalence. `Annulus` and `CoverModels` transport this exact generator to the actual overlap, not merely to an abstract cyclic group.

5. **Disconnected overlap/right side handled explicitly.** `CoverModels` proves homeomorphisms from the full disk and annulus coproducts onto the actual cover subspaces. Locally constant coordinate indices are proved constant along every path and groupoid arrow. Component paths are constructed using disk/annulus path-connectedness. The component inclusion induces an actual based fundamental-group equivalence, proved by coordinate reconstruction of paths. Therefore every vertex group in the full interior groupoid is trivial, even though the union is generally disconnected, and every full-overlap anchor vertex group is generated by its concrete half-circle loop. No connected-overlap or connected-interior hypothesis is imposed.

## Abstract bridge and its geometric application

`GroupoidQuotient.pushout_vertex_quotient_of_component_data` is a proved bridge, not an axiom encoding the target. Its inputs are a Cat pushout, left-object injectivity, a basepoint outside the overlap, left connectedness, indexed component paths, trivial right vertex groups, arrow-invariant component indices, and cyclic overlap vertex groups.

The kernel calculation has both directions: commutativity and trivial right vertex groups kill transported relators; a normalized based functor into the quotient by their normal closure extends through the Cat pushout and detects the reverse kernel inclusion. Surjectivity is separately derived by gluing natural transformations, encoded as functors into an arrow category. A basepoint-normalized contraction makes the resulting quotient reconstruction cover every target loop. Neither surjectivity nor kernel equality is a bridge assumption.

`GeometricPushout.neighborhood_inclusion_vertex_quotient` discharges every listed input with the constructed geometry: actual `coverPushout`; subtype-object injectivity; the original basepoint outside all interiors; connectivity from the proved neighborhood homotopy equivalence; coordinate component paths and indices; proved trivial interior vertex groups; and proved generation of overlap anchor groups. The index lift `ULift.{u} ι` only reconciles universes, and its relator range is proved equal to the original range.

Finally, `RelatorTransport` constructs anchor paths using the specified whiskers and the inverse radial trajectory. The retraction of that trajectory is a constant path, and the half-circle generator retracts to the prescribed attaching-circle loop. Thus `neighborhoodPi1Equiv_attachingLoopClass` identifies the exact requested conjugated relators. `Main` transports established exactness along the actual neighborhood inclusion equivalence, uses the proved factorization of `inclusionPi1`, and builds the compatible quotient equivalence by the first isomorphism theorem. There is no merely noncanonical group isomorphism substituted for the induced map.
